# Input Protocol

This document defines the standard interaction protocol for the PPT Cognitive Design Skill framework.

The framework performs best when user requirements are structured clearly.

Presentation intelligence depends heavily on:

high-quality communication input.

---

# Core Principle

Better presentation generation begins with:

better presentation understanding.

The system should collect:

- audience information
- presentation objectives
- cognitive requirements
- narrative expectations
- visual preferences
- communication constraints

before generating presentation structures.

---

# Standard Input Structure

Users are encouraged to provide information using the following structure:

```text
Presentation Scenario:
Audience Type:
Presentation Duration:
Primary Objective:
Content Domain:
Technical Complexity:
Preferred Style:
Risk-control Concerns:
Existing Materials:
Special Requirements:
```

---

# Input Dimension Explanation

## 1. Presentation Scenario

Examples:

- teaching interview
- undergraduate lecture
- thesis defense
- scientific conference
- business pitch
- interdisciplinary presentation

Purpose:

determine reasoning mode
and presentation strategy.

---

## 2. Audience Type

Examples:

- undergraduate students
- graduate students
- expert academics
- interdisciplinary committee
- executives
- public audience

Purpose:

determine cognitive density
and explanation strategy.

---

## 3. Presentation Duration

Examples:

- 5 minutes
- 15 minutes
- 30 minutes
- 1 hour

Purpose:

determine pacing,
slide count,
and narrative compression.

---

## 4. Primary Objective

Examples:

- teaching demonstration
- scientific communication
- persuasion
- funding acquisition
- interdisciplinary positioning
- knowledge explanation

Purpose:

determine narrative priorities.

---

## 5. Content Domain

Examples:

- agriculture
- GIS
- remote sensing
- AI
- medicine
- engineering
- ecology

Purpose:

determine terminology systems
and visual semantics.

---

## 6. Technical Complexity

Examples:

- low
- medium
- high
- interdisciplinary

Purpose:

determine abstraction tolerance
and compression strategy.

---

## 7. Preferred Style

Examples:

- minimal
- academic
- teaching-oriented
- business-professional
- scientific storytelling
- visually restrained

Purpose:

determine visual style systems.

---

## 8. Risk-control Concerns

Examples:

- reduce questioning risk
- avoid excessive technical exposure
- simplify mechanisms
- reduce cognitive overload

Purpose:

activate adaptive constraint systems.

---

## 9. Existing Materials

Optional inputs:

- papers
- figures
- screenshots
- diagrams
- datasets
- previous slides

Purpose:

support multimodal presentation reasoning.

---

## 10. Special Requirements

Examples:

- bilingual presentation
- undergraduate-friendly explanation
- interdisciplinary positioning
- low-density slides
- visual-first communication

Purpose:

enable fine-grained adaptation.
