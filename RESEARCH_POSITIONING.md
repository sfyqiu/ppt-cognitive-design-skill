# Research Positioning

This document defines the research positioning of the PPT Cognitive Design Skill framework.

The framework is NOT intended to be:

- a slide beautification tool
- a template repository
- a decorative presentation generator
- a simple PPT automation system

The framework is designed as:

an AI-native cognitive presentation intelligence system.

---

# Core Research Objective

The framework studies:

how AI systems can transform
complex information
into cognitively efficient presentation structures.

The central research problem is:

```text
How can AI optimize presentation design
for audience cognition,
understanding,
and communication efficiency?
```

---

# Research Domains

The framework integrates concepts from:

- cognitive psychology
- human-computer interaction
- educational communication
- visual semantics
- narrative intelligence
- explainable AI
- audience modeling
- adaptive reasoning systems

---

# Core Research Hypothesis

Presentation quality is primarily determined by:

- cognitive efficiency
- audience adaptation
- narrative continuity
- visual semantic clarity
- knowledge compression quality

NOT:

visual decoration complexity.

---

# Research Contributions

## 1. Cognitive Presentation Intelligence

The framework treats presentation generation as:

- cognitive reasoning
- communication engineering
- semantic visual structuring

rather than template beautification.

---

## 2. Multi-agent Presentation Architecture

The framework introduces:

- teaching agents
- academic agents
- business agents
- scientific storytelling agents

Different scenarios activate different reasoning systems.

---

## 3. Audience-centered Presentation Generation

The framework dynamically adapts:

- density
- pacing
- terminology
- hierarchy
- explanation depth

based on audience cognition.

---

## 4. Knowledge Compression Intelligence

The framework models presentation design as:

cognitive compression.

Complex knowledge is transformed into:

understandable visual communication systems.

---

## 5. Explainable Presentation AI

The framework introduces:

transparent presentation reasoning.

The system explains:

- why layouts are selected
- why narrative structures are used
- why simplification occurs
- why visual systems are applied

---

## 6. Constraint-aware Presentation Optimization

The framework actively prevents:

- cognitive overload
- visual chaos
- audience mismatch
- narrative instability

through reasoning constraints.

---
