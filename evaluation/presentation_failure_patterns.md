# Presentation Failure Pattern System

This system defines common presentation failure patterns from a cognitive perspective.

The goal is to help AI systems detect:

- cognitive overload
- narrative failure
- visual confusion
- audience mismatch
- presentation fatigue

Presentation intelligence requires understanding failure.

---

# Core Principle

A presentation fails when:

the audience struggles to understand.

Failure is usually caused by:

- excessive complexity
- unstable hierarchy
- poor narrative structure
- visual competition
- audience mismatch

---

# 1. Cognitive Overload Failure

## Characteristics

- excessive text
- dense layouts
- too many simultaneous concepts
- excessive terminology
- crowded visuals

## Audience Symptoms

- reading fatigue
- loss of attention
- reduced comprehension
- cognitive exhaustion

## AI Optimization Strategy

- simplify content
- split slides
- increase whitespace
- reduce terminology density
- emphasize key takeaways

---

# 2. Narrative Failure

## Characteristics

- disconnected sections
- abrupt transitions
- weak progression
- information dumping
- unclear storytelling

## Audience Symptoms

- confusion
- loss of orientation
- reduced engagement
- poor memory retention

## AI Optimization Strategy

- strengthen transitions
- establish narrative rhythm
- improve progression logic
- simplify storyline

---

# 3. Visual Hierarchy Failure

## Characteristics

- multiple focal points
- equal-weight elements
- chaotic eye movement
- unstable alignment
- inconsistent emphasis

## Audience Symptoms

- attention confusion
- visual fatigue
- slow comprehension
- scanning difficulty

## AI Optimization Strategy

- establish dominant focal regions
- reduce competing visuals
- improve alignment
- strengthen hierarchy contrast

---

# 4. Audience Mismatch Failure

## Characteristics

- excessive abstraction
- inappropriate terminology
- wrong pacing
- over-technical explanation
- insufficient explanation

## Audience Symptoms

- disengagement
- misunderstanding
- intimidation
- loss of interest

## AI Optimization Strategy

- adapt terminology
- simplify explanation
- adjust pacing
- increase intuitive visuals

---

# 5. Scientific Communication Failure

## Characteristics

- isolated data stacking
- unclear mechanism explanation
- fragmented visual systems
- weak contribution emphasis

## Audience Symptoms

- unclear scientific meaning
- reduced credibility
- weak takeaway retention

## AI Optimization Strategy

- emphasize mechanism explanation
- improve figure storytelling
- strengthen contribution visibility
- improve scientific continuity

---

# 6. Teaching Presentation Failure

## Characteristics

- formula overload
- abstract-first teaching
- dense academic language
- low visual explanation

## Audience Symptoms

- low understanding
- reduced participation
- cognitive frustration

## AI Optimization Strategy

- increase visual teaching
- simplify terminology
- improve progressive explanation
- reduce abstraction

---

# 7. Business Presentation Failure

## Characteristics

- weak value communication
- slow messaging
- dense analytical explanation
- weak emotional pacing

## Audience Symptoms

- reduced persuasion
- low engagement
- unclear business value

## AI Optimization Strategy

- simplify messaging
- emphasize value quickly
- strengthen emotional rhythm
- reduce cognitive friction

---

# 8. Visual Fatigue Failure

## Characteristics

- continuous dense slides
- repetitive layouts
- visual monotony
- excessive decorative noise

## Audience Symptoms

- attention decline
- reduced retention
- visual exhaustion

## AI Optimization Strategy

- alternate visual rhythm
- increase breathing space
- diversify slide pacing
- reduce unnecessary decoration

---

# Final Principle

A presentation succeeds when:

the audience understands comfortably.

A presentation fails when:

understanding requires excessive cognitive effort.
